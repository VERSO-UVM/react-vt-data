Why we built this

Vermont has the data.
Finding it is another story.

Information about Vermont communities exists across agencies, reports, databases, and geographic datasets. These sources are valuable individually, but they can be difficult to connect and integrate into the same conversation.

The Vermont Data Collaborative brings these pieces together into accessible tools that make our community information easier to explore, compare, and use for evidence-supported policies.

Behind the platform

Our Data Process.

Here at the VDC, we believe public data becomes more useful when it can be found, connected, and understood. That is why we ensure that our data is findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable (FAIR).

01

Collect

Gather public datasets from federal, state, and local sources.

02

Standardize

Clean and structure data so information from different sources can work together.

03

Integrate

Connect datasets through common geographies, identifiers, and measures.

04

Explore

Make the resulting information available through maps, analysis tools, and downloadable data.
