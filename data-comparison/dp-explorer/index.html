Note on year coverage

Census Data Profile variable labels (categories, subcategories, variable names) change between ACS vintages. A selection may only cover a subset of years if its label was introduced, renamed, or restructured in a particular release. Variables with broad year coverage (e.g. racial composition totals, median age) are generally labeled consistently; highly specific sub-groups may only appear in one or two vintages.
