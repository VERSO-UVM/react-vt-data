Why We Built This

Communities need reliable information to make decisions, but important data often exists across disconnected agencies, reports, and databases.

The Vermont Data Collaborative integrates demographic, housing, infrastructure, environmental, and policy data into a single platform designed to support planning, research, and public decision-making.

Our Mission

Empower Vermont communities with accessible, transparent, and actionable data that supports evidence-based decision making.
