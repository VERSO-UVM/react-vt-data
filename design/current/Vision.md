# Overview 
The stated purpose of the Vermont Data Collaborative is to help Vermonters leverage data to make better and more informed decisions, particularly in a rural context, but in any fashion.

# Specifics

## Minimum Viable Product
1. Customizable, automated town reports highlighting key information about a town:
    * visualizations and tables based on location and `persona` of user: 
        * what geographic areas the person is interested in
        * what **topic** areas the person is interested in: demographic, labour, zoning, etc. 
2. Exploratory mapping and searching: finding areas that make specific groups work well together. 
3. Customized Reports, more detailed reports, on specific areas, with two pilots:
    * Community Health
    * Housing
